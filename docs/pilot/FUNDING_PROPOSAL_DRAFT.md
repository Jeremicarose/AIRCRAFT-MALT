# Funding Pre-Discussion: Receiver Operator Product Pilot

This is a draft for community review, not a submitted or approved funding
proposal. Confirm the current CKB Community Fund DAO process before publishing.

## Short request

Fund a bounded six-person product-validation pilot for CKB Receiver Registry V2.
The programme will pay experienced ADS-B / Mode S receiver operators and network
coordinators to test whether CKB-backed identity and lifecycle evidence solve a
real cross-network coordination problem.

Requested budget: **$2,800 USD equivalent**, converted using the funding method
and exchange-rate rule accepted when the proposal is submitted.

## Why

The contract, SDK, evidence tools, and reference application are implemented.
The missing evidence is not another feature. It is whether independent receiver
operators and coordinators understand the system, experience the underlying
problem, and would continue testing or integrating it.

Unpaid general feedback is unlikely to produce a balanced technical cohort. The
study asks for 85-120 minutes, wallet setup for owner tasks, and a seven-day
follow-up. A completion incentive respects that time and reduces selection bias
toward people already involved in CKB.

## Why now

- The Registry lifecycle and immutable testnet deployment have reproducible
  evidence.
- The first-time usability remediation and automated browser coverage are
  complete locally.
- The product now states what CKB proves and what remains an operational MLAT
  responsibility.
- No external operator pilot has run, so adoption and product value remain
  unproven.

## Scope

The funded cohort contains:

- four receiver owners or operators;
- two network coordinators or developers; and
- two qualified backups for cancellations.

The study covers Registry registration, discovery, update, lifecycle evidence,
export, MLAT-reference interpretation, and a seven-day return task. It does not
claim live MLAT accuracy, hardware attestation, production security, or aviation
safety suitability.

## Payment gates

1. Milestone 1 is accepted only when the readiness report names the exact source
   commit, deployment outpoint, contract hash, hosted URL, and successful wallet
   rehearsal.
2. Milestone 2 is accepted only when six pseudonymous session records show role
   eligibility, task outcomes, interventions, and incentive status.
3. Milestone 3 is accepted only when the public report includes funnel metrics,
   task results, negative findings, follow-up outcomes, and the product decision.

No milestone depends on participants praising the product.

## Public deliverables

- source revision and automated test results;
- pilot page, one-pager, application questions, consent text, and task scripts;
- anonymized recruitment and completion counts;
- redacted session evidence where permission allows;
- severity-ranked usability and adoption findings;
- spending summary; and
- continue, change, or stop recommendation.

## Smaller sponsorship options

If the full request is not appropriate:

- **$240 participant pool:** six completion incentives plus two backup reserves;
- **$900 safe-launch pool:** independent review, wallet rehearsal, and hosting;
- **in-kind support:** independent binary review, private form/scheduling tools,
  hosted infrastructure, or introductions to qualified receiver communities.

Ring-fenced support does not imply endorsement of the product's conclusions.
