# Interview Guide

Ask for examples from the participant's real work. Keep questions neutral and
allow negative answers.

## Existing problem

- Tell me about the last receiver replacement, transfer, move, or retirement.
- How was the receiver identified in each system?
- Which people and systems had to coordinate?
- What went wrong, or what could not be verified?
- How often does this happen?

## Current workaround and cost

- How do you solve this today?
- What parts work well and should remain?
- How much time or engineering effort did the last change require?
- Did it create operational risk, maintenance work, or a trust problem?
- Who has authority to change the current record?

## Product reaction

- What problem here is familiar to you?
- Which part, if any, would save time or reduce risk?
- Which part duplicates your existing network?
- What was confusing or unnecessary?
- What did you expect the product to prove that it did not prove?
- Did the MLAT investigation make the Registry identity more useful or merely
  add a demonstration?

## Adoption

- Would this fit your current workflow? Why or why not?
- What prevents you from using it?
- Is a cross-network identity useful, or are network-specific identifiers enough?
- Is the wallet and transaction cost acceptable?
- What API, SDK, event, export, authentication, or service behavior is missing?
- Who would decide whether to integrate it? Who could block it?

## Continuation and commercial potential

- Would you use this again in another test?
- Would you evaluate an SDK or network integration?
- Could your organization integrate, sponsor, partner with, or pay for this?
- What evidence or change must exist first?
- What one action can happen within seven days?
- Would you introduce another person who manages receivers?

Do not describe praise as adoption. Commercial potential requires a real
problem, decision relevance, product fit, continued interest, and a concrete
next action.
