## Purpose

You are invited to test CKB Receiver Registry, an experimental tool for stable
ADS-B and Mode S receiver identities. We are studying whether receiver owners
and network coordinators find identity, ownership, lifecycle history, discovery,
and export useful in their real work.

The purpose is product research. This is not an aviation-safety study, hardware
certification, investment offer, or request for operational receiver access.

## What participation involves

- preparation of about 5-15 minutes;
- one moderated remote product session of up to 45 minutes;
- one interview of up to 20 minutes; and
- one short task or decision follow-up within seven days.

Receiver owners may approve transactions on CKB Pudge testnet in their own
wallet. Testnet tokens have no intended monetary value. Coordinators can complete
directory and export tasks without a wallet.

## Information collected

The study may record:

- your role and non-sensitive receiver or network experience;
- task completion, elapsed time, errors, questions, and facilitator assistance;
- your feedback, objections, integration needs, and follow-up decision;
- public testnet transaction identifiers created for the study; and
- screen or audio recording only if you separately approve it below.

Names and contact details are stored privately and separately from the public
research record. Public records use a pseudonymous participant ID.

## Information never requested

Do not provide a private key, seed phrase, password, exchange login, feed key,
private feed URL, IP address, exact receiver location, or access to a physical
receiver. Use fictional or deliberately coarse coordinates for test records.

## Public and permanent testnet data

Registry transactions are public. A test record's label, declared coordinates,
capabilities, owner lock, status, and transaction history may remain publicly
inspectable after the session. Revocation is permanent and does not erase prior
transactions. Do not enter personal information or an exact private location.

## Risks and limitations

- The software is experimental, testnet-only, and not independently audited for
  production use.
- Wallet setup or transactions may fail or take longer than expected.
- Public testnet records cannot be treated like private form responses.
- The Registry does not prove physical hardware, location truth, feed honesty,
  clock accuracy, or observation truth.
- Replay or simulation data may be used to demonstrate the MLAT workflow. It is
  labeled and is not live aircraft evidence.

You may pause or stop at any time. Refusing a question does not affect any
payment already earned under the stated completion policy.
