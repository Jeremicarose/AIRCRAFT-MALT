# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Operators, engineers, and internal reviewers validating a distributed MLAT stack, especially people inspecting receiver coverage, aircraft activity, and hybrid live-versus-simulated system behavior.

## Product Purpose
Provide an operational control surface for a CKB-backed MLAT system so users can verify receiver discovery, inspect aircraft movement, and understand current system mode without reading logs or raw API responses.

## Brand Personality
Technical, composed, aviation-native.

## Anti-references
Toy map demos, generic crypto dashboards, over-gamified telemetry UIs, and loud neon hacker visuals that reduce trust in operational data.

## Design Principles
- Put the map first, because airspace context is the product's anchor.
- Make system state obvious at a glance, especially mode, connectivity, and receiver coverage.
- Prefer operational density over decorative chrome.
- Treat simulation and live infrastructure as first-class states, not hidden implementation details.
- Keep the interface credible on both desktop and mobile review surfaces.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Target WCAG AA contrast, preserve keyboard access for controls, maintain readable density on small screens, and avoid motion that is required to understand state.
