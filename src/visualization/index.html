How to read the walkthrough

The story should be obvious before you touch anything else.

The first screen should answer four things quickly: what MLAT means on this map, whether you are seeing replay or live traffic, why aircraft selection is the first action, and what receiver selection teaches afterward about support and coverage.

01

Open the map

The dashboard opens as a hosted walkthrough with clear mode labels so visitors know whether they are looking at replay traffic or a live feed.

02

Click an aircraft

Aircraft focus surfaces the recent path, altitude, uncertainty, and receiver count so the estimate feels explainable instead of abstract.

03

Click a receiver

Receiver focus shifts the story to coverage and contribution so visitors can understand how the network supports what they just saw on the map.

What the walkthrough teaches

The product becomes easier to understand when the map does the explaining.

Rather than leading with infrastructure, the walkthrough starts with one aircraft estimate on the map. From there, receivers explain support and placement, while mode labels keep replay versus live context explicit.

Aircraft selection Shows the latest position, a short history, and how many receivers supported that estimate.
Receiver selection Shows where station-side coverage comes from and how that network relates back to the map story.
Mode labeling Keeps hosted replay and live context obvious before anyone tries to interpret the traffic.
