# Code Of Conduct

## Expected Conduct

Project discussions and reviews must remain professional, specific, and focused
on the work. Contributors are expected to:

- treat participants with respect regardless of background or experience
- critique code, evidence, and claims rather than people
- disclose conflicts of interest relevant to reviews or grant claims
- protect private keys, credentials, security reports, and personal information
- accept maintainer decisions without harassment or repeated disruption

## Unacceptable Conduct

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized content, deliberate
misrepresentation, doxxing, credential exposure, and sustained disruption are
not accepted in repository spaces or project events.

## Reporting And Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer identified in
[MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md). Security vulnerabilities must instead follow
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

The maintainer may edit or remove content, limit participation, or ban a
participant when necessary to protect contributors and project integrity. When
the maintainer is involved in a complaint, resolution requires a mutually
agreed independent reviewer. No response-time SLA is currently offered.
